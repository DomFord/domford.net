Based on a sampling of promotional material from toolmakers, developers, modmakers and game publishers, I look at how the software for producing AI-generated dialogue for nonplayer characters in digital games is promoted. What is promised to whom and by whom? What are the tensions between those different promises, audiences and realities? I observe three paradoxes or tensions: revolutionary in effect, yet seamlessly integrated; endless, tailored content, yet creatives are still supreme; and characters shaped by the player, yet also just like 'real' people.
